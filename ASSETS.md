# Asset provenance

## Research images

- `assets/images/flight.webp`: original raster image embedded in the user-provided `RAL_strider_final.pdf`, page 1. Extracted without the paper layout or figure annotations. Converted to WebP for delivery.
- `assets/images/platform.webp`: original raster image embedded in the same PDF, page 6. Extracted without the paper layout or figure annotations and resized for delivery.
- `assets/images/simulation.webp`: `resources/images/mujoco_capture.png` from `Milkomedia/STRIDER_MPC_MuJoCo`, commit `437607700aa24e63676c53abab28668683e817b2`. Resized and converted to WebP.
- `assets/images/plot-ui.webp`: `resources/images/viewer_capture.png` from the same commit. Resized and converted to WebP.

The research imagery belongs to its original rights holders. No license for those images is assigned by this website package. No AI-generated research imagery or invented results are included.
